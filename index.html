Role Design & Research

Track Challenge

"Design and present a digital product or experience that reimagines the workplace environment for employees. This may include ways to combat employee burnout, disengagement, wellbeing, and lack of connection in a hybrid/remote work environment. This does not have to be exclusively for the Digital & Technology industry but should be focused on an office or commercial space."

Secured 2nd place out of 30 teams (100+ participants) in the CBRE Company Track at the UT Dallas Design-a-thon Challenge. My team and I conducted rapid UX research, prototyping, and design for a real-world CBRE business problem.

It was an intense, fun, and rewarding experience of rapid research, design, and presenting! Huge kudos to my teammates Dung Nguyen and Ajith Anand for their amazing work and dedication. 👏

Thank you to CBRE, the UX Club at UT Dallas, and everyone involved in hosting this amazing event!

Our Solution

A computer application that helps managers monitor and support employee wellbeing through data-driven insights, gamified productivity, and a virtual workplace where teams can connect, collaborate, and prevent burnout together.
